## Full Description (4000 chars)
**Storyteller Magic** makes bedtime something your child actually looks forward to.

Pick a theme. Choose a tone. Set the length. A personalized bedtime story comes to life in seconds — with your child's name woven in from the very first word.

**100+ stories, fully offline.** The local story library works without internet. New collections added regularly.

**Interactive branching.** Let your child choose the path. Simple, calm decisions that keep them engaged without screen overload.

**Ambient soundscapes.** Gentle rain, soft fire, or a quiet forest hum plays under the story. Feels like being read to.

**Built for sleep.** Dark interface, slow fade animations, adjustable reading speed, and a sleep detection that fades out when little eyes close.

**Streaks and badges.** Kids earn badges as they read — First Tale, 7-Night Streak, Adventurer — making bedtime feel like an achievement.

**Parent tools.** Usage reports, parental gate, multiple child profiles, reading history.

Languages: English & Turkish
Requires: Android 7.0+
