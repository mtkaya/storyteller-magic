## Full Description (4000 chars max)
**Storyteller Magic** turns bedtime into a magical experience your child will look forward to every night.

Choose a theme, pick a tone, and select how long the story should be. In seconds, a unique bedtime tale comes to life — crafted around your child's name and their favorite kind of adventure.

### ✦ What makes Storyteller Magic different

**Library-first.** Over 100 stories available offline, no internet needed. New themed collections are added regularly.

**Interactive stories.** Some tales let your child choose the path — "help the little fox" or "follow the star trail." Simple, calm choices that keep them engaged without overstimulating.

**Ambient sound.** Gentle rain, crackling fire, or a soft forest breeze plays underneath the story, making the experience feel truly immersive.

**Personalized from the start.** Enter your child's name during setup — every story greeting, hero tag, and reading streak will feel like it was made just for them.

**Sleep-friendly design.** Dark mode by default. Soft animations. Reading speed control. Sleep detection that fades out when your child drifts off.

**Reading progress that motivates.** Track streaks, unlock badges, and watch your child build a real reading habit — one bedtime at a time.

### 📚 Story Library
- Adventure, Friendship, Magic, Nature, Courage, Calm, and more
- Interactive branching stories
- Short (5–8 min) and longer (10–15 min) options
- Turkish and English support

### 👨‍👩‍👧 For Parents
- Parental controls and usage reports
- Multiple child profiles
- No ads. No random content. No screen-addiction traps.
